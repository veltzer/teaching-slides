# Phishing: Don't Take the Bait
---

## What is Phishing?

- Phishing is a type of cyber attack where criminals attempt to steal sensitive information like login credentials or financial information.
- Phishers use fraudulent emails, texts, websites, and other tactics to trick victims into revealing this information.
- Phishing attacks often impersonate legitimate companies, organizations, or individuals.

---

## How Phishers Lure Victims

- Phishing emails and messages typically:
  - Appear to come from a trusted source
  - Create a sense of urgency
  - Ask you to update or verify account information
  - Offer an enticing deal or reward
- Malicious links and attachments can install malware on your device.

---

## Types of Phishing Attacks

- Email Phishing
- Spear Phishing (targeted)
- Smishing (SMS/text message phishing)
- Vishing (voice/telephone phishing)
- Angler Phishing (social media)
- Pharming (redirecting to fake websites)

---

## Tips to Avoid Phishing

- Be wary of unsolicited messages or requests for personal information.
- Verify the sender's email address and URLs for legitimacy.
- Don't click on links or open attachments from untrusted sources.
- Use multi-factor authentication for accounts when possible.
- Keep software and security updates current.
- Report suspected phishing attempts.

---

## Technical Tools to Combat Phishing

- Email filters and spam blockers
- Web browser security extensions/plugins
- Antivirus and anti-malware software
- Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)
- Password managers
- Security awareness training

---

## Email Filters and Spam Blockers

- Help identify and block suspected phishing emails
- Use advanced techniques like header analysis, URL scanning, and content filtering
- Many email providers have built-in spam filtering capabilities
- Third-party spam filter services are also available

---

## Web Browser Security Extensions

- Browser extensions designed to detect and block phishing sites
- Analyze URLs, website content, and check against blocklists
- Examples: Google Safe Browsing, Bitdefender TrafficLight, Web Of Trust
- Be cautious and only use extensions from trusted sources

---

## Antivirus and Anti-Malware Software

- Protect against malware that may be delivered through phishing
- Can scan emails, websites, and files for malicious content
- Reputable options: Malwarebytes, Kaspersky, Norton, etc.
- Keep definitions updated for the latest threat protection

---

## Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)

- Encrypt internet traffic and mask your IP address
- Helps prevent man-in-the-middle attacks and eavesdropping
- Use a reputable, secure VPN service provider
- Enable the VPN when connecting to public Wi-Fi networks

---

## Password Managers

- Store and manage login credentials securely
- Generate strong, unique passwords for accounts
- Reduce the risk of reusing passwords across sites
- Examples: LastPass, 1Password, KeePass

---

## Security Awareness Training

- Educate employees on recognizing phishing threats
- Simulate phishing attacks to test awareness
- Promote security best practices and policies
- Ongoing training reinforces vigilance against evolving threats

---

## Staying Safe is Key

- Phishing attacks continue to increase in sophistication.
- Being cautious and verifying legitimacy is crucial.
- Follow security best practices to protect your information.
- Utilize technical tools to enhance your phishing defenses.
- If you suspect a phishing attempt, report it immediately.
