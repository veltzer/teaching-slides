# Formjacking
---
## What is Formjacking?

Formjacking, also known as Web Form Skimming or Digital Skimming, is a type of cyber attack where malicious code is injected into the important/payment forms of legitimate websites to steal sensitive information, such as credit card details and personal data.

---

## How Does Formjacking Work?

1. The attacker compromises a website or third-party service (e.g., web analytics, chat service) used by the target website.

2. The attacker injects a malicious script into the website's payment form or checkout page.

3. When a user submits their payment information on the compromised website, the malicious script captures the sensitive data and sends it to the attacker's server.

4. The stolen data can be used for fraudulent activities, such as making unauthorized purchases or selling it on the dark web.

---

## Consequences of Formjacking

- Financial losses for individuals and businesses
- Identity theft and fraud
- Loss of customer trust and damage to reputation
- Potential legal liabilities and regulatory fines
- Disruption of business operations

---

## Mitigating Formjacking

- Implement Content Security Policy (CSP) to restrict script sources
- Use Subresource Integrity (SRI) to verify the integrity of third-party scripts
- Regularly monitor and update third-party services and libraries
