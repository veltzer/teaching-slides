Emerging roles in AI-assisted development:

- **Primary Focus**: Ensuring quality and correctness of AI-generated solutions

## Risk Management for AI Future

Anticipating and managing risks in AI-driven development:

```markdown
# AI Future Risk Management Framework

## Technological Risks
### AI Tool Dependency
- **Risk**: Over-reliance on specific AI tools or vendors
- **Mitigation**: Diversified tool portfolio, vendor independence strategies
- **Monitoring**: Regular assessment of tool dependency levels

### Quality Degradation
- **Risk**: Reduced code quality due to insufficient AI validation
- **Mitigation**: Enhanced validation frameworks, human oversight requirements
- **Monitoring**: Continuous quality metrics tracking

## Human Capital Risks
### Skill Atrophy
- **Risk**: Loss of fundamental development skills
- **Mitigation**: Regular skill maintenance programs, manual coding requirements
- **Monitoring**: Periodic skill assessments and capability testing

### Change Resistance
- **Risk**: Team resistance to AI adoption and evolution
- **Mitigation**: Change management programs, gradual adoption, success demonstration
- **Monitoring**: Regular team sentiment surveys and adoption metrics
```
