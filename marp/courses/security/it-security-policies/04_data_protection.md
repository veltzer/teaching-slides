# Data Protection and Privacy

## Why Data Protection Matters
- Data is one of the most valuable assets your organization holds
- Customer trust depends on how you handle their information
- Regulatory fines for data breaches can be devastating
- Every employee handles data - this applies to you

---

## Data Classification

## Not All Data Is Equal

| Level | Label | Examples | Handling |
|-------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | **Public** | Marketing brochures, press releases | Share freely |
| 2 | **Internal** | Org charts, internal memos | Keep within the company |
| 3 | **Confidential** | Financial reports, contracts | Share on need-to-know basis |
| 4 | **Restricted** | Customer PII, medical records, credit cards | Strictest controls |

- Know the classification of the data you work with
- Handle each level according to company policy

---

## Personally Identifiable Information (`PII`)

## What Counts as `PII`?
- Full name combined with:
    - Social security / national ID number
    - Date of birth
    - Home address
    - Phone number
    - Email address
    - Financial account numbers
    - Medical information
    - Biometric data

- If it can identify a specific person, treat it as `PII`

## Encryption Basics

## Encryption in Your Daily Work

- **Email encryption** - use it for sensitive communications
- **Full disk encryption** - enabled on your company laptop (verify with IT)
- **File encryption** - encrypt sensitive files before sharing
- **`HTTPS`** - the padlock icon in your browser means the connection is encrypted
- **Encrypted messaging** - use company-approved messaging tools

## Secure File Sharing

## Do This, Not That

| Instead of... | Use... |
|---------------|--------|
| Email attachments with sensitive data | Company-approved file sharing platform |
| Personal cloud storage (`Google Drive`, `Dropbox`) | Corporate cloud storage |
| USB drives | Secure file transfer tools |
| Printing sensitive documents | Screen sharing in meetings |
| Texting passwords | Password manager sharing feature |

---

## Secure File Sharing Checklist

- Use company-approved platforms only
- Set appropriate permissions (view-only vs. edit)
- Set expiration dates on shared links
- Use password protection for external shares
- Verify recipient email addresses before sharing
- Remove access when no longer needed
- Never share to personal email accounts

## `GDPR` - General Data Protection Regulation

## Key Principles (EU Regulation, Global Impact)
- **Lawfulness** - you must have a legal basis to process personal data
- **Purpose limitation** - collect data only for specified purposes
- **Data minimization** - collect only what you need
- **Accuracy** - keep personal data up to date
- **Storage limitation** - do not keep data longer than necessary
- **Security** - protect data with appropriate measures

---

## `GDPR` Rights of Individuals

- **Right to access** - people can ask what data you hold about them
- **Right to rectification** - people can ask you to correct errors
- **Right to erasure** - the "right to be forgotten"
- **Right to data portability** - people can take their data elsewhere
- **Right to object** - people can opt out of certain processing

## Fines
- Up to 20 million euros or 4% of annual global revenue
- Whichever is higher

---

## `CCPA` - California Consumer Privacy Act

## Similar Rights for California Residents
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt out of data selling
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

## Why It Matters Even Outside California
- Many companies apply `CCPA` standards globally
- Other US states are passing similar laws
- The trend is toward stronger privacy protections everywhere

## Data Protection in Practice

## Everyday Scenarios

- **Receiving a customer data request**: forward to your privacy team immediately
- **Creating a spreadsheet with customer data**: classify it as Confidential or Restricted
- **Disposing of old files**: use secure deletion, not just the recycle bin
- **Working from home**: ensure family members cannot see your screen
- **Discussing customer details**: never in public spaces

---

## Mobile Device Security

## Your Phone Is a Computer
- Company email, files, and apps on your phone are at risk
- Lost or stolen phones are a major source of data breaches

## Protection Measures
- Enable screen lock (PIN, fingerprint, or face recognition)
- Enable remote wipe capability
- Keep your operating system and apps updated
- Only install apps from official stores
- Do not jailbreak or root your device
- Use company `MDM` (Mobile Device Management) if required

---

## Removable Media and Storage

- USB drives are easily lost or stolen
- They can carry malware between systems
- Company policy on removable media:
    - Use encrypted USB drives only (if approved)
    - Never plug in unknown USB devices
    - Transfer files through approved cloud platforms instead
    - Report lost USB drives containing company data immediately

## Data Retention and Disposal

- Do not keep data "just in case"

## Key Takeaways

- Know the classification level of the data you handle
- Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit
- Use only company-approved tools for sharing files
- Understand `GDPR` and `CCPA` basics and how they affect your work
- Protect mobile devices as carefully as your laptop
- Follow data retention and secure disposal policies
- When in doubt about data handling, ask your privacy team
