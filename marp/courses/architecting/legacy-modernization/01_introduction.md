# Introduction to Legacy Modernization

---
## What This Chapter Covers

- What legacy means
- Why modernize

---
## What Legacy Means

- Code that still earns money
- Hard to change safely
- Built on outdated assumptions
- Often older than the team

---
## Why Modernize

- Risk reduction
- Hiring difficulty
- Compliance pressure
- Cost of change rising

---
## When Not to Modernize

- Product nearing retirement
- Stable and cheap to run
- No strategic upside
- Risk outweighs reward

---
## Signs of a Legacy System

- Single deploy expert
- No tests
- Manual releases
- Tribal knowledge
- Vendor lock-in

---
## Hidden Risks

- Compliance debt
- Security debt
- Data quality debt
- Operational debt

---
## Why It Got This Way

- Short-term wins
- Team turnover
- Acquired systems
- Quiet maturity
